# Appearance
----
A Tan and Brown Catfolk, standing tall at 6'5". He has a slim, striped tail and wears simple civilian clothing, including a hood, a scarf, and brown fingerless gloves, but no shoes. His outfit features shades of blue, brown, and tan. He has striking amber-golden cat eyes, a short snout, sharp fangs, and a keen tracking nose. His magical abilities manifest in deep purple and black hues that flow through his veins, causing his eyes to glow when activated. The fur on his fingertips rises when channeling his magic, with purple and black energy crackling around him. For visual reference, please see the picture below.
