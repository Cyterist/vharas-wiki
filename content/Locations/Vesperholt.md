At the heart of the known world stands Vesperholt, a grand and crumbling metropolis built upon the bones of forgotten ages. The city’s spires reach toward the dimming sky, its streets tangled in layers of past civilizations. Each district tells a story of conquest, tragedy, and decay.


# Known Locations
---
## The Ever-Fall Market
A sprawling bazaar where the wealth of ruined kingdoms is sold. Here, merchants peddle relics stolen from Hollowed lands, and whispers tell of treasures that should have never been unearthed.

## The Ashen Keep
A fortress ruled by the Sable Lords, a secretive aristocracy who claim descent from the Ember God itself. Their blood runs gold, and they alone have the power to command the dying light, forging it into weapons and armor.

## The Drowned Quarter
Once a prosperous district, now half-sunken into the obsidian waters of Lake Mourn, where spirits sing beneath the waves, luring the desperate and the grieving to their deaths.
## The Red Basilica 
A church built upon a cathedral that came from nowhere—one day, it simply stood where an entire district had been. Within its walls, a choir of faceless priests sings hymns to The Crimson Saint, a being of unknown origin who promises salvation through the embrace of darkness.
