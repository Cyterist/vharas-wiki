Thornhold is normally a peaceful grove, they allow people to travel through and have always been helpful. but as of about 1 and a half weeks ago in game a bunch so corruption from the hollowing has sprouted and spreading up north past Vesperholt in random spots and no one knows why, this has sent the radiant crusade into a panic of sorts, having to pull men off the front and draft people from many cities to contain the spread. during this you have heard rumors that the crusade may be attacking the grove for an unknown reason. this has caused the druids to seal the grove for the first time in a very long time
