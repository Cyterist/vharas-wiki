Unlike the rest of the pantheon, the Crimson Saint is still growing in power. Once mortal (or so the legends claim), it preaches a new world born in blood. The Saint’s followers are fanatics, known as The Red Choir, who believe only through war and sacrifice can the world be reborn.

Doctrine: The gods have failed. Burn the old world, forge a new one in its place.

Rituals: Ritualized duels, marking the forehead with three drops of blood, keeping a journal of one’s sins to be “burned away” upon death.
