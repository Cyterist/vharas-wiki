6 Ancient tyrants who refused to die and became gods. Each Sable Lord rules a kingdom lost to time, their castles existing in fractured echoes of history. Worshiped by desperate nobles who seek to prolong their own rule.

Doctrine: Power is eternal. To rule is divine.

Rituals: Blood oaths upon obsidian thrones, branding one’s name into their flesh to ensure history does not forget them
