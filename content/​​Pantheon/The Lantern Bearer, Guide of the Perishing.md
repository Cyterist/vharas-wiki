A wandering god, said to walk the roads of Vharas, guiding lost souls. Its followers, known as The Wayfarers, light candles in windows, hoping their god will watch over travelers.

Doctrine: No road should be left in darkness. To travel is to seek.

Rituals: Carrying a lantern at all times, leaving offerings of light (candles, oil, silver mirrors) at crossroads
