The last god one meets upon death. The Pale Warden weighs a soul’s burdens before allowing them to pass. Those deemed unworthy are sent back into the world, cursed to linger until they atone.

Doctrine: Judgment is absolute. None may pass without reckoning.

Rituals: Carving the names of the dead onto bone, binding the souls of murderers into iron locks until justice is served
