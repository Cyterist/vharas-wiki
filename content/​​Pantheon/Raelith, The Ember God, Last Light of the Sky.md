Once the greatest of the gods, the Ember God is now a fading star, its body shattered and bleeding embers upon the land. The Sun-Bearers carry its dying flame, preserving the last sparks of divinity. Some believe it can be restored, others claim it is better to let it die.

Doctrine: Keep the flame alive at all costs. Protect the last light from being consumed by the Hollowing.

Rituals: Burning sacred incense, carrying ember-forged weapons, reciting the Hymns of the Last Dawn at twilight.
