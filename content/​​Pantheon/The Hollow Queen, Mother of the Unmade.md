The Hollow Queen is not worshiped, but surrendered to. She is the whisper that calls from the Hollowing, offering peace in dissolution. Her followers, the Silent Ones, erase their own names, believing identity is a burden.

Doctrine: You are nothing. Release your past, surrender to the void.

Rituals: Wearing white veils to signify a “half-death,” inscribing names onto stone before casting them into the Hollow.
