A sorrowful god, Velmara tends to the dead, ensuring their passage into whatever lies beyond. Some say she was once mortal, a widow whose grief made her divine. Those who fear being forgotten call upon her name.

Doctrine: Mourn the dead, honor the past. Ashes are sacred, for they are all that remains.

Rituals: Wearing veils of soot during funerals, whispering the name of a lost loved one into a candle flame.
