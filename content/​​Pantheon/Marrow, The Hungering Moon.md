A god of cycles, Marrow devours to create. Some fear it, others revere it, believing hunger is sacred. Worshiped by starving villages, warlords, and those who trade in flesh. Those who follow Marrow say, “What is taken must be given.”

Doctrine: Life is hunger. To take is to feed, to starve is to die. Feast when the moon is full, sacrifice when it is dark.

Rituals: Bloodletting under the full moon, fasting before battle, wearing ivory masks during sacred feasts.
